Cat Survival Shop Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 20, 2026

Cat Survival Shop is an educational game developed by KitagawaGameDev.

Information We Collect

Cat Survival Shop does not collect, store, or transmit personal information from players. The game does not require an account and does not collect names, email addresses, location information, photographs, contacts, or other personal information.

Children's Privacy

Cat Survival Shop is designed for children, including young children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Advertising and Analytics

Cat Survival Shop does not contain advertisements and does not use third-party advertising, analytics, or tracking SDKs.

Internet Access

Cat Survival Shop does not require an internet connection to play and does not transmit player information to external servers.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

If this privacy policy changes, the updated version will be made available at this URL.
